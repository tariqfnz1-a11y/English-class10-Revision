Grammar practice

Attempt the MCQs. Choose one answer, then press Check. Review the explanation before moving to the next question.

Quick grammar checklist

  • Identify the subject before choosing a verb.
  • Check the tense and time expression.
  • For passive voice, use the correct form of be + past participle.
  • For narration, check pronouns, tense, time words and sentence type.
  • Check capital letters and punctuation.

Translation practice

Translate the Urdu sentences into English before opening the model answers. Answers may vary if the meaning and grammar are correct.

Translation checklist

  • Identify the subject and verb.
  • Choose a tense that matches the meaning.
  • Check articles, prepositions and word order.
  • Preserve the original meaning instead of translating mechanically.
  • Check spelling and punctuation.

Poetry revision

Time

Revise the central idea, speaker's message, imagery and vocabulary using the exact prescribed poem.

Practice: Explain how the poem presents the value or effects of time. Support your answer with accurate details from the textbook.

The Road Not Taken

Revise the speaker's choice, imagery of roads, reflection and possible interpretations.

Practice: Explain how the two roads help develop the poem's ideas. Check the actual lines before quoting.

Poetry answer checklist

  • Identify the poem and poet as printed in the textbook.
  • Explain the context if asked.
  • Explain the stanza in clear, simple language.
  • Identify imagery or a figure of speech where relevant.
  • State the central idea without inventing quotations.

Essay, paragraph and written expression

Essay planning framework

  1. Introduction: introduce the topic.
  2. Body paragraph 1: main point and example.
  3. Body paragraph 2: further explanation or solution.
  4. Body paragraph 3 if appropriate: effects or recommendations.
  5. Conclusion: summarise the central message.

Possible practice topics

  • Importance of Education
  • My Aim in Life
  • Environmental Protection
  • Benefits and Risks of Technology
  • Cleanliness in Our School
  • Value of Time
  • Pakistan and Responsible Citizenship
